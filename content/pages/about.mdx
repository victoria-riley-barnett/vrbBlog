Hi, I'm **Victoria** - I designed and teach an interdisciplinary [machine learning course](#post-interdisciplinary-machine-learning) at San Francisco State University. It is designed to generalize critical representation, computing complexity, and model evaluation concepts to life scientists, social scientists, and other non-computer scientists.

Before teaching, I worked in [R&D data operations](#post-r-and-d-data-operations) for a physical-AI company. I also build and contribute to [open-source software](https://github.com/victoria-riley-barnett/), especially if I see a way to simplify things for myself (and hopefully others :)

## Interests & Skills
- programming theory ~ [recursive grammars](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~jshutt/thesis/rags.html) and evaluation, functional & systems programming, and language design
- sharing information ~ open-source software, knowledge graphs, technical documentation systems, and teaching
- ml ~ evaluation and interpretability
