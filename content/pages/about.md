Hi, I'm **Victoria**. I teach machine learning at San Francisco State
University. Before that I spent six months in R&D data operations for a physical-AI company, and before that I was in the U.S. Air Force.

I build open-source tools, mostly for reading, note-taking, and personal
infrastructure: a Tailscale plugin for KOReader, a Calibre replacement that runs on an e-reader, and a small language and machine called Cairn.

I studied computer science at SFSU. I live in San Francisco. In my free time I read, play Paradox map games, take walks around the city, and play with my terminal.
