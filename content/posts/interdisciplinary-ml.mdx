I designed and teach CSC 411: Intermediate Machine Learning at San Francisco State University, in the Promoting Inclusivity in Computing minor. The course sits between the sequence's most data-science-heavy course and its most compute-heavy one, CSC 511: Protein Modeling with Deep Learning, and assumes no calculus, linear algebra, or programming beyond the introductory course.

The aim is to carry representation, computational complexity, and model evaluation to life scientists, social scientists, and other students who will use machine learning rather than build it. Every operation is derived and implemented by hand before a library does it, so students leave with a working transformer encoder they wrote themselves.

I plan to release the course materials under a permissive license after the semester ends.
