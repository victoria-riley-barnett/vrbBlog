I designed and teach CSC 411, an interdisciplinary machine learning course at San Francisco State University. I plan to release the course materials under a permissive license sometime following the end of the semester. The course is designed to generalize critical representation, computing complexity, and model evaluation concepts to non-computer scientists as part of a sequence. It is positioned between the most data science intensive course of the sequence and the Deep Learning Protein folding course, the most computer intensive course of the sequence. It is designed to be accessible to life scientists, social scientists, and other non-computer scientists.
