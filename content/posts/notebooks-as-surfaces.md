In class, the best way to teach my students about machine learning is being able to visualize both the operations and the math, in turn, abstractly, as well as literally, rendered by the other form.

By this I mean: when we look at a tensor, they do not yet see machine learning. But if they see machine learning, they can now truly see the tensor. Teaching affords translation through this system of interaction, establishing a "regime of context", as all sequences do.
