Before teaching, I spent seven months working in R&D for a physical-AI company. As a Data Coordinator and Shift Supervisor placed through Ascen Workforce LLC, I worked on scaling an experimental pilot platform through hardware revisions and into multi-site production, refining the experimental content of the data through rapid iteration and feedback and into multiple applications. 

Alongside this, I led significant documentation, quality, and operations process improvements into the broader organization as a result of my program, using analysis and visualization drawn from telemetry and user feedback to inform (and argue for) changes. 

Extensive administrative, logistics, and scheduling experience from my time in USAF test and community organizing served me extremely well in this role, which required all of the above exercised in concert with a single staff engineer and a small team of collectors.
