The false object of capacity essentializes an individualist perspective: your job is necessarily to never be at capacity, because you must socialize. There is no work that is "above your capacity", because that would mean the idea is still too trapped in your head.

Alignment is generated from shared consensus, that is, from touch points we can agree on. If we cannot align, necessarily our touch points are misaligned. A touch point is co-determined with reality and the social field, so we can strive for consciousness of both in order to generate our touch points.

There is no planning problem, there is no capacity problem, there is no amount of people to convince, and there is no secret organizing technology. There is only our shared socialization, and so we must do it in such a way that the shared socialization becomes the common point of the shared consciousness.
