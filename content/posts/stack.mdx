This site is built with **Astro**, a static site generator whose sites are neither static nor dynamic - it uses Vite (effectively a [website compiler](https://vitejs.dev/)) but can choosily hydrate slices of the page called [Islands](https://docs.astro.build/en/concepts/islands/), giving us a bit of a compromise for apps that only need a tiny bit of interactivity. I am hosting with [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/).
