## [Tailscale for KOReader](https://github.com/victoria-riley-barnett/koreader-tailscale)

<img src="/assets/projects/koreader-tailscale.png" alt="Tailscale plugin menu in KOReader on an e-reader" loading="lazy">

A Lua plugin for KOReader that brings VPN support to Kindle, Kobo, and PocketBook ARM e-ink devices. Paired with Syncthing and an iOS shortcut, it's my replacement for Calibre and Send to Kindle; my library is distributed, rolling, and always available. Seven releases, outside contributors, and 223 stars.

## [CSC 411: Intermediate Machine Learning](https://github.com/victoria-riley-barnett/intermediate-ml)

<img src="/assets/projects/intermediate-ml.png" alt="Lecture slide on evaluation from CSC 411" loading="lazy">

The course I designed and teach at SF State for the Promoting Inclusivity in Computing (PINC) program. It is machine learning pedagogy that follows a strategy of:
1. theoretical foundations adequate for;
2. domain application
2. & practical experience in that domain application.

One way we do this is by working with representations they encounter in their domain, and an emphasis on the historical impetus of a particular technique. This allows us to build both a generalized and contemporary understanding of the field without necessarily preparing these students to be Machine Learning engineers.

## [SerialPM](https://github.com/victoria-riley-barnett/serialpm)

<img src="/assets/projects/serialpm.jpg" alt="SerialPM project timeline view" loading="lazy">

Front end of a project-management app with organisations, tasks, team management, and real-time chat. Built as a team capstone at SF State. I led 5 engineers through the full software lifecycle process. The backend was built against real AWS resources, and as such, were degraded after the course ended. However, I have a front-end showcase (React, Vite, Tailwind) with a timeline view, task management, and chat, and the backend API can be examined in the repo. 

The project was built with a focus on a visual continuous timeline view of a user's scoped project work. The system can support multiple agile methodologies, organizations, teams, and calendar inputs.

## IDE (in development)
